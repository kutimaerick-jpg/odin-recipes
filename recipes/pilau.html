Beef Pilau

Description

Beef Pilau is a tasty rice dish cooked with beef, spices, onions, and herbs. It is a popular East African meal known for its rich flavor and wonderful aroma.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 500g beef (cut into cubes)
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 teaspoon pilau masala
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 4 cups water or beef broth
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Boil the beef until tender and keep the broth.
  3. Heat oil in a cooking pot and fry the onions until golden brown.
  4. Add garlic and ginger paste, then cook for 1 minute.
  5. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  6. Add the beef, pilau masala, cumin, black pepper, and salt.
  7. Stir well and cook for 5 minutes.
  8. Add the rice and mix evenly with the spices and beef.
  9. Pour in the beef broth or water and bring to a boil.
  10. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice is fully cooked.
  11. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.
