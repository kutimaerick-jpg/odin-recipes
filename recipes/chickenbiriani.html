Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken Biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with chicken, spices, herbs, and basmati rice. It is popular in many parts of the world and is known for its rich aroma and delicious taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add garlic, ginger paste, and tomatoes. Cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Add chicken, yogurt, turmeric, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
  5. Cook the chicken until tender.
  6. Boil the rice until it is partially cooked and drain the water.
  7. Layer the rice over the cooked chicken mixture.
  8. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.
