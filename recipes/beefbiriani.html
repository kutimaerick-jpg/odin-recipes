Beef Biryani

Description

Beef Biryani is a rich and flavorful rice dish made with tender beef, aromatic spices, herbs, and basmati rice. It is a popular meal known for its delicious taste and amazing aroma.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g beef (cut into small pieces)
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 20 minutes.
  2. Boil the beef until tender and set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  4. Add garlic, ginger paste, and tomatoes. Cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the boiled beef, yogurt, turmeric, chili powder, cumin, garam masala, and salt.
  6. Cook the mixture for 10 minutes to allow the flavors to combine.
  7. Boil the rice until partially cooked and drain the water.
  8. Layer the rice over the beef mixture.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.
